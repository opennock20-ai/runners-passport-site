GOOD TO KNOW

The little details.

How much do the cards cost?

Each personalised race card is £4.99. For UK card-only orders, our planned shipping is 99p for a single card and free delivery for two or more. Final pricing will be confirmed at launch.

Can I use my own race photograph?

Absolutely. Upload a photo and add your result and personal caption in our card personaliser. The current site is a preview: photos stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Are the cards ready to order?

We're perfecting the print finish and final artwork. The website currently lets you build a preview and explore the basket; payment and real orders are not yet enabled.

What races will you offer?

We're starting with a small UK collection, including the Great North Run and major marathons. More events will follow as we validate the artwork and permissions.
